Editions is a macOS application for photographic curation and presentation. This policy explains how the application handles your photographs and project information, and what happens when you contact Summers Media or use TestFlight.

Photographs and local project data

Editions references photographs in locations you choose. It keeps file-access bookmarks, file identities, filenames and paths, metadata, thumbnails and previews in its local sandbox. Your original photographs remain in place. Curatorial decisions, collections, sequences, project notes, competition briefs, reasoning and decisions, print captions, print and wall designs, production settings and saved analysis evidence are stored on your Mac. App preferences also retain appearance, introduction and lesson progress, and backup status.

Image measurements, Apple Vision image similarity and optional Apple aesthetics scoring run locally. Editions does not upload your photographs or projects to a Summers Media service, use a remote image model, include analytics or advertising SDKs, or require an Editions account. Foundation Models integration is not enabled.

The optional guided sample copies six credited public photographs into an app-owned local folder. It does not copy your personal originals. Removing a photograph from a project or deleting its print design does not delete the original source photograph.

Exports and backups

Exports are written to a destination you choose. A production package includes the prepared photograph and chosen project, design and output metadata. Its manifest excludes original filesystem paths and access bookmarks. Review PDFs may include selected filenames and your project notes according to your export choices. You control any later sharing of those files.

Library backups contain project records, notes, decisions, metadata, original paths and access references, and saved analysis. They exclude original photographs and disposable thumbnail caches. Automatic backups are created in the app's local storage; Editions does not operate a cloud backup service. If you copy or share a backup, its stored metadata and references go with it. Keep independent backups of original photographs and important library snapshots.

Support and beta feedback

Contact david@summers.media for privacy questions, support or beta feedback. Information you choose to send, including your email address, message and attachments, is received through the email or feedback service you use and is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please send only information needed for your request, and review screenshots and attachments for private photographs, names or paths before sharing them.

Help → Copy Support Information copies a limited app, operating-system, architecture and capability summary to your clipboard. It excludes project names, notes, photograph names and paths, access bookmarks, identifiers and image contents. It does not send the summary automatically.

Support correspondence may remain in Summers Media's email or feedback records after a request is resolved. You can contact Summers Media at the address above to ask about information you have voluntarily sent or request its deletion. The application's local removal controls do not delete correspondence or feedback already sent through other services. Apple manages retention and deletion of information held by TestFlight under its own privacy practices.

TestFlight

When Editions is distributed through TestFlight, Apple separately collects beta crash and usage information and shares it with the application provider. Feedback submitted through TestFlight is also shared and can include screenshots and contact information. This platform behavior is distinct from Editions' local image processing. Review Apple's TestFlight privacy information before joining a beta or sending feedback.

This website and external links

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or external fonts. GitHub processes information about website requests as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting this page is separate from processing photographs in Editions.

Links you choose to open, such as the public sample-source catalog, open external websites whose own privacy practices apply. Your email service and any service you choose for sharing exports or backups also have their own practices.

Your control

You choose the files and directories Editions can access and where to keep originals, exports and copied backups. You can edit or remove project membership and authored decisions through the app, manage automatic backups in Settings, and choose whether to use the guided sample or optional Apple score. These local actions do not remove copies you previously exported, backed up or shared.

Local library information stays on your Mac until you remove it or replace the library. Thumbnail and preview caches are disposable and can be rebuilt from accessible originals. When automatic backups are enabled, Editions keeps the 10 most recent valid automatic snapshots; manual or copied backups remain until you remove them. You can revoke file and directory access using macOS controls. Contact support for help locating local Editions data before removing it, and preserve any work you want to keep.

Policy changes

Summers Media may update this policy as Editions changes. The effective date above identifies the current policy. Privacy and support questions can be sent to david@summers.media.
